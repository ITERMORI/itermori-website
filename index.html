About us

A Lebanese company serving education and society.

Itermori works across education, training, research and publishing. We create and manage educational programmes, organise courses, conferences and exhibitions, and provide educational consultancy to schools, teachers, administrators and students.

We partner with ministries, municipalities, donors, universities and companies to develop skills, promote citizenship and social responsibility, and bring innovation, including artificial intelligence, into learning.
